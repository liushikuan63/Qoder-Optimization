# Post-Deployment Shareables

Optional, non-intrusive follow-up after a verified upload or publish.

## Boundaries

- Offer at most once; if declined, do not follow up.
- Never publish or send anything on the user's behalf.
- Use only information the user supplied for this release.
- Remove account names, internal URLs, customer data, screenshots with identifiers, and any secret.

## Deliverables

- A short visual card or summary suitable for the team.
- Paste-ready copy that states what shipped, what was verified, and what remains unverified.
- A clear note when the release is still pending review or was only uploaded to a trial environment.
